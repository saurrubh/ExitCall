Exit Call lets you simulate an incoming call at a time you choose. Add a caller name and an optional number, then preview the screen or schedule the call.

Choose a preset or design your own screen. Move the caller name, number, avatar, and controls. Set colors, fonts, labels, button dimensions, and tap or swipe actions for answering, declining, and ending the call.

Repeat a call every minute until you tap Stop. On compatible devices, System Phone lets the default Phone app show the incoming call after you enable Exit Call in Calling accounts.

Press Volume Up twice to schedule a call in 10 seconds. The optional Accessibility service lets you use the shortcut from another app.

The simulation runs on your device. Lock-screen delivery depends on your Android settings.
